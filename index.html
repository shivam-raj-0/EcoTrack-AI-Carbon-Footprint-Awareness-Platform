Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the total amount of greenhouse gases generated by human activities.

Why should I reduce my carbon footprint?

Reducing emissions helps fight climate change and protects the environment.

How can I reduce emissions?

Use renewable energy, public transport, and energy-efficient appliances.
